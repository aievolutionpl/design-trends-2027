# Application recipes

Read the selected numbered entry only. Colors below are starting palettes, not contrast guarantees; test actual pairings. Adapt each to the existing brand. For detailed source and implementation context, see [video directions](video-directions.md), [additional directions](additional-directions.md) and [sources](sources.md).

## 1. Handmade / analog
- **Decision and use:** choose for craft-led food, editorial or artisan brands when a physical artifact is relevant; if authenticity cannot be established, use a labelled illustration instead.
- **Composition:** one photographed cutout hero, clean aligned headline and offer panel; generous clear space.
- **Color:** warm paper `#F4EBDD`, ink `#222222`, brick `#9B3C2F` accent.
- **Typography:** a licensed expressive display serif with a neutral readable sans for details.
- **Imagery:** scan or photograph authorized paper, paint or thread; keep copy editable. Substitute a labelled digital placeholder if absent.
- **Motion:** optional gentle reveal of the cutout; static equivalent under reduced motion.
- **Anti-pattern:** generic artificial grain everywhere or claiming a digital imitation is handmade.
- **QA:** check material rights, provenance, contrast on paper, crop and offer legibility at final size.
- **Brief example:** bakery poster with actual packaging scan, clear price and collection details.

## 2. Cute character
- **Decision and use:** choose for friendly education or onboarding when an audience welcomes a mascot, not for masking serious risk.
- **Composition:** one character silhouette beside a conventional labeled action; use a small expression sheet and consistent proportions.
- **Color:** cream `#FFF6E7`, navy `#183153`, coral `#D9574F` accent.
- **Typography:** rounded display face with clear neutral sans for instructions.
- **Imagery:** create or commission an original character, plus small-size and monochrome versions; avoid unlicensed lookalikes.
- **Motion:** a short optional reaction to an action, never required to understand an error; provide static state.
- **Anti-pattern:** mascot replaces labels, hides a warning or interrupts the task.
- **QA:** test recognition small, tone with intended users, controls and non-character access to messages.
- **Brief example:** language-learning onboarding with one mascot and a prominent real start button.

## 3. Process / imperfection
- **Decision and use:** select for portfolios or launch stories with authorized real milestones; otherwise choose a different lead.
- **Composition:** finished result first, followed by three chronological artifacts and concise decision captions.
- **Color:** off-white `#F5F3EE`, charcoal `#252525`, blue `#315A96` for annotations.
- **Typography:** editorial serif for the result and legible sans for timestamps and annotations.
- **Imagery:** approved actual sketches and iterations; omit confidential material and label AI involvement honestly.
- **Motion:** optional linear step transition, with all stages accessible statically.
- **Anti-pattern:** fabricated sketches or false before/after evidence.
- **QA:** verify permissions, dates and captions; ensure the result is understood without the backstory.
- **Brief example:** brand case study showing three real decision points with client-approved images.

## 4. Found real textures
- **Decision and use:** choose when material supports a product story, such as a fabric maker or local venue.
- **Composition:** one large texture field alongside a quiet solid information block.
- **Color:** stone `#E9E3D8`, black `#1D211F`, forest `#315848` accent, adjusted to the real material.
- **Typography:** sturdy sans heading, readable regular sans body; avoid small type on the texture.
- **Imagery:** photograph authorized relevant material; retain a master and optimize exports. A missing texture is a labeled placeholder, not a real scan.
- **Motion:** optional slow crossfade to a detail; use a still fallback.
- **Anti-pattern:** random stock grain or repeating a noticeable tile behind text.
- **QA:** inspect tiling, image rights, actual-size contrast and file weight.
- **Brief example:** textile identity using a licensed fabric photograph and clear product labels.

## 5. Surrealism
- **Decision and use:** use one impossible premise to explain a campaign idea; avoid misleading product evidence.
- **Composition:** single focal object with consistent perspective and separate headline/CTA area.
- **Color:** pale sky `#D8EAF2`, dark ink `#182831`, orange `#C84F2D` as focal accent.
- **Typography:** plain bold sans headline and restrained body so the image carries the surprise.
- **Imagery:** composite licensed images or render an object at impossible scale; disclose synthetic scenes where misunderstanding is likely.
- **Motion:** optional single transformation, not several competing effects; provide the key still.
- **Anti-pattern:** unrelated visual tricks, inconsistent shadows, fictitious installation presented as real.
- **QA:** inspect product fidelity, lighting, rights, legibility and whether viewers understand the premise.
- **Brief example:** conceptual campaign showing an oversized teacup floating above a city, explicitly an illustration.

## 6. Expressive typography
- **Decision and use:** choose for a short memorable message or cultural identity, not text-heavy reading.
- **Composition:** oversized headline as hero; align date, body and action on a quieter grid.
- **Color:** near-white `#F8F7F2`, near-black `#161616`, electric blue `#254AC2` accent.
- **Typography:** custom or licensed display lettering for headline, neutral text face for details; verify required glyphs including Polish.
- **Imagery:** typography can be the image; add at most one simple supporting graphic and preserve live accessible text.
- **Motion:** optional staggered reveal only after a static readable headline exists.
- **Anti-pattern:** decorative body copy, illegible wraps or unlicensed fonts.
- **QA:** check optical spacing, glyphs, license, mobile wraps and headline recognition at intended size.
- **Brief example:** festival identity built around a short Polish headline and clear event information.

## 7. Deliberately naive
- **Decision and use:** choose for grassroots events or comic irreverence when the audience expects it; this is the video's more speculative proposal.
- **Composition:** one intentionally awkward decorative layer around a clean aligned information block.
- **Color:** paper `#FFF9DB`, blue `#204EC6`, red `#C83737` with black text.
- **Typography:** plain system sans with an oversized blunt headline; use no more than one playful display treatment.
- **Imagery:** original simple clip art or properly licensed artwork; do not imply false amateur provenance.
- **Motion:** optional single playful pop; static version conveys the same content.
- **Anti-pattern:** treating poor accessibility or broken UX as an aesthetic.
- **QA:** have a reader locate date, location and action at final size; check contrast and rights.
- **Brief example:** community gig flyer with one starburst and a legible date/location block.

## 8. Dynamic materials
- **Decision and use:** choose for floating navigation when layering supports orientation, not for dense body copy.
- **Composition:** opaque content plane with one elevated translucent toolbar; clear boundary and focus state.
- **Color:** dark ink `#18202B`, pale surface `#F3F5F7`, blue `#315BB5` action; tune translucent overlay to content.
- **Typography:** restrained interface sans with stable body sizes and high-contrast labels.
- **Imagery:** use background imagery only if authorized; verify the toolbar against light, dark and changing regions.
- **Motion:** subtle state transition, optional; provide static opaque fallback and reduced motion.
- **Anti-pattern:** blur on every card or transparent text over uncontrolled content.
- **QA:** test contrast on actual backgrounds, `backdrop-filter` fallback, keyboard focus and rendering cost.
- **Brief example:** travel website with a floating navigation bar above photography and solid reading panels.

## 9. Expressive geometry
- **Decision and use:** choose to make a brand system recognizable across a site, packaging and graphics without inventing novel controls.
- **Composition:** define two shape families and a consistent spacing grid; reserve the strongest form for primary emphasis.
- **Color:** ivory `#F8F4EB`, ink `#1B2529`, teal `#006B65` accent.
- **Typography:** confident geometric display face paired with a neutral interface sans.
- **Imagery:** create original simple vector motifs; retain conventional semantic buttons with labels and focus.
- **Motion:** optional shape transition between states, respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- **Anti-pattern:** decorative shapes that look interactive or color-only status.
- **QA:** verify shape consistency, readable labels, touch targets, focus and recognition across sizes.
- **Brief example:** B2B platform identity using two repeatable corner shapes and conventional dashboard controls.

## 10. Purposeful motion
- **Decision and use:** choose for digital narratives, transitions or feedback when change over time clarifies meaning; for print use another lead.
- **Composition:** complete static layout first, then animate only the element relevant to the user's action.
- **Color:** neutral surface `#F5F5F2`, ink `#20252A`, blue `#2455A4` for interactive emphasis.
- **Typography:** stable, readable sans; don't animate body text in a way that prevents reading.
- **Imagery:** licensed images or original vector layers; never hide essential content waiting for animation.
- **Motion:** define one consistent transition for orientation; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, feature-detect enhancements, preserve an equivalent static outcome.
- **Anti-pattern:** scroll hijacking, perpetual motion or animation as the only feedback.
- **QA:** test reduced motion, keyboard access, content without animation, browser fallback and low-powered devices.
- **Brief example:** editorial site where a diagram transitions between steps while the full static explanation remains accessible.


## 11. Playful retro computing
- **Decision and use:** technology editorial or events seeking playful historical references; avoid imitating operating-system controls in functional workflows.
- **Composition:** modular pixel grid, one oversized cursor or window and a separate readable information area.
- **Color:** cream `#F7F1DE`, cobalt `#2042A0`, orange `#E76822`; verify contrast.
- **Typography:** licensed bitmap display for short labels, neutral sans for body and Polish glyphs.
- **Imagery:** original pixel motifs or licensed retro assets; do not present fictitious data as real.
- **Motion:** optional single cursor movement; keep static equivalent.
- **Anti-pattern:** fake interactive windows or inaccessible pixel body type.
- **QA:** test labels, focus, recognition, contrast and actual-size legibility.
- **Brief example:** exhibition poster about early computing with clear event details.

## 12. Quiet editorial
- **Decision and use:** long-form reading, independent publishing or considered storytelling; not the best lead for energetic festival promotion.
- **Composition:** disciplined grid, one image and generous negative space with a clear reading order.
- **Color:** ivory `#F2EDE1`, forest `#1D3830`, charcoal `#202521`; test text/background pairing.
- **Typography:** licensed expressive serif headline and highly legible body type; check Polish characters.
- **Imagery:** one authorized photograph or clearly labelled synthetic still life.
- **Motion:** optional quiet transition; complete static reading experience.
- **Anti-pattern:** low contrast, tiny type or empty space that hides navigation.
- **QA:** test actual contrast, text sizing, responsive layout, reading order and image rights.
- **Brief example:** independent journal cover with single paper sculpture and prominent title.

## 13. Ornamental maximalism
- **Decision and use:** cultural editorial, hospitality or beauty with a deliberately rich tone; avoid as lead for urgent data-heavy tasks.
- **Composition:** one focal still life, 2–3 recurring ornamental motifs, and a quiet separate copy zone.
- **Color:** burgundy `#692834`, cream `#F5EADA`, silver `#B9B9B5`; check contrast.
- **Typography:** licensed ornamental display for a short title, neutral body font with Polish glyphs.
- **Imagery:** original or licensed lace, flowers and silverware; synthetic materials are not proof of craft.
- **Motion:** optional slow reveal of one detail with static equivalent.
- **Anti-pattern:** decoration behind essential small text or copying cultural motifs without context and permission.
- **QA:** check hierarchy at final size, provenance, licensing, image crop and contrast.
- **Brief example:** boutique fragrance editorial with rich objects and a clean information strip.


## 14. Data poetry
- **Decision and use:** editorial stories grounded in a verifiable dataset; decorative data-like patterns must be explicitly labelled as illustration.
- **Composition:** one flowing field of points and lines with a separate legend and a calm reading area.
- **Color:** navy `#18243A`, cream `#F5EBDD`, coral `#D95C50`; verify contrast.
- **Typography:** neutral legible sans for labels and source, expressive display for the title.
- **Imagery:** map actual values with documented units and scale; never claim synthetic marks are real data.
- **Motion:** optional sequential reveal, with the complete static graphic and data table available.
- **Anti-pattern:** invented patterns presented as measured observations or decorative axes without units.
- **QA:** validate source, mapping, scale, labels, uncertainty, color-independent distinctions and alt text.
- **Brief example:** city mobility story with a cited dataset and accessible summary table.

## 15. Biomorphic sculpture
- **Decision and use:** nature-inspired identity, product editorial or cultural campaign; avoid implying artificial objects are real products.
- **Composition:** two or three rounded asymmetric volumes with one dominant silhouette and open space for copy.
- **Color:** moss `#536C4D`, cream `#F1EBDD`, clay `#A65F49`; test text contrast.
- **Typography:** clear humanist sans for body and a restrained display serif.
- **Imagery:** original sculptural render or authorized photograph; disclose synthetic material where relevant.
- **Motion:** optional slow shape transition; preserve a static version.
- **Anti-pattern:** claiming a generated object is physically manufactured, sustainable or an actual material sample.
- **QA:** verify image provenance, silhouette at small size, legibility, contrast and any environmental claims.
- **Brief example:** a fictional design exhibition poster with a labelled synthetic sculptural study.

## 16. Monochrome architecture
- **Decision and use:** high-contrast cultural editorial or architecture story; avoid when color is necessary to differentiate critical states.
- **Composition:** architectural light and shadow intersect a disciplined modular grid with a distinct information plane.
- **Color:** black `#111111`, white `#FAFAF7`, mid-gray `#777777`; check each text pair.
- **Typography:** licensed grotesk display face with a readable sans and required glyphs.
- **Imagery:** authorized architecture photographs or clearly identified synthetic architectural abstractions.
- **Motion:** optional short reveal of a shadow; maintain equivalent static content.
- **Anti-pattern:** low-contrast gray copy or using a synthetic building as evidence of a real location.
- **QA:** check actual text contrast, crop, rights, readability, and non-color state indicators.
- **Brief example:** a monochrome architecture journal cover with a separate legible title block.
